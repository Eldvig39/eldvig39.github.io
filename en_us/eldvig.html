Eldvig program is designed to calculate:
- windings of three-phase asynchronous electric motors with a power of 1 to 100 kW and more;
- windings of phase rotors;
- windings of armatures of collector machines (electric motors and generators) of direct current with a power of up to 10 kW;
- windings of armatures and excitation windings of direct and alternating current micromotors with a power of up to 1 kW;
- windings of pull-in coils of relays, starters, contactors, etc.
- iIn the Eldvig program, you can check the correctness of the calculation of the existing winding of a three-phase asynchronous electric motor;
- In the Eldvig program, you can simulate the operation of a three-phase asynchronous electric motor under various loads changed by the User with the definition of the main parameters - efficiency, cos (f), sin (f), total, active and reactive power, etc.;
- In the Eldvig program, you can create an estimate for materials for the repair of asynchronous electric motors - with a squirrel-cage and phase rotor, as well as synchronous electric motors - with a rotor on permanent magnets or on electromagnets.
This is only a part of the Eldvig program capabilities. For more detailed information about all the program capabilities, see EldvigFeatuгes.pdf.

The Eldvig program is designed to calculate:
- windings of three‑phase asynchronous electric motors with power from 1 kW to 100 kW and above;
- windings of single‑phase asynchronous electric motors with main and starting windings, where the starting winding is disconnected after startup;
- windings of two‑phase asynchronous capacitor motors with a permanently connected capacitor;
- windings of wound‑rotor motors;
- armature windings of commutator machines (electric motors and generators) operating on direct current, with power up to 10 kW;
- armature and field windings of DC and AC micromotors with power up to 1 kW;
- windings of pull‑in coils for relays, starters, contactors, etc.
- The Eldvig program allows you to verify the correctness of the winding calculation for an existing three‑phase asynchronous electric motor;
- The Eldvig program enables simulation of a three‑phase asynchronous motor’s operation under various loads adjustable by the User, determining key parameters such as efficiency, cos(φ), sin(φ), apparent, active, and reactive power, etc.;
- The Eldvig program can be used to prepare a materials cost estimate for repairing asynchronous motors (with squirrel‑cage or wound rotors) as well as synchronous motors (with permanent magnet or electromagnet rotors).

Calculation of three‑phase asynchronous motors with squirrel‑cage rotors is based on a methodology (with significant modifications) described in the book by G. K. Zherve, “How to Calculate the Stator Winding of an Asynchronous Motor,” State Energy Publishing House, Moscow–Leningrad, 1960, as well as using other literature sources.
The program is written in Java and is cross‑platform — it can run on Windows and Linux operating systems.
