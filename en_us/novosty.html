12. Since 12.02.2025, support for the English language (USA) has been added to the Eldvig program.

11. Since 16.12.2024, support for the Portuguese language (Brazil) has been added to the Eldvig program.

10. Since 03.10.06.2021, the ability to create an estimate for materials for the repair of synchronous, asynchronous, phase-rotor electric motors, etc. has been added to the Eldvig program.

9. 06.09./2021 the ability to create winding diagrams for three-phase asynchronous electric motors has been added to the Eldvig program: The number of stator slots is up to 168, the number of parallel branches is up to 12, the number of slots per pole and phase is an integer. Winding types:
- with concentric coils: two-tier and three-tier;
- single-layer: with the ability to separate the stator core, without the ability to separate the stator core, staggered and chain;
- two-layer: loop.

8. On 14.01.2019, the Yakor program was included in the Eldvig program. Now, in the Eldvig program, you can calculate anchors and excitation windings of collector electric machines.

7. 12/17/2018 Eldvig 3.76 was released. Several new wires were added, both domestic and imported. The "Fill the slot with wires of different diameters" sub-item was added to the "Calculators" menu item, which allows you to fill with several wires of different brands and diameters. Minor errors were fixed, blue frames were added to the parameter selection forms.

6. Eldvig 3.75 was released on 10.12.2018. Bugs were fixed, in particular, the display of squares instead of letters was eliminated.

5. Since 25.08.2016, the Yakor program for calculating the windings of the anchors of DC and AC collector electric machines has been posted on the website.
